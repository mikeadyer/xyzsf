Cable cars

The California Street line runs past the hotel. A single ride is $9 and has no transfers, so use a pass if you ride more than once or twice. Lines can be long at the Powell St turnaround.

Cable car info

Fares and paying

Adult single ride $2.85 with Clipper, MuniMobile or a tapped card ($3 cash). Day pass $5.70. Visitor Passports cover Muni and cable cars: 1 day $15, 3 days $35, 7 days $47. Youth 18 and under ride Muni free, but not cable cars.
